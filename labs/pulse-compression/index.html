Key idea: pulse compression separates energy duration from compressed delay width. A long wideband waveform can carry energy for a long time while its matched-filter response remains narrow, approximately set by bandwidth.

Try this: hold B at 80 MHz and sweep T from 0.5 to 10 µs. The raw envelopes change dramatically, while the normalized compressed width stays nearly fixed. Then increase B and watch the compressed peaks narrow.
