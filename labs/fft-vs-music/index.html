What this lab isolates: the conventional spectrum is limited by the finite array aperture. MUSIC instead asks whether a steering vector lies in the estimated signal subspace and becomes nearly orthogonal to the noise subspace.

Important limitation: this is an idealized subspace demonstration. It assumes two uncorrelated sources, correct model order, a full-rank two-source signal subspace, accurate calibration, and effectively perfect covariance knowledge. In real data, finite snapshots, low SNR, source coherence, model mismatch, and calibration errors can broaden, shift, or destroy MUSIC peaks.
