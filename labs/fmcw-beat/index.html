τ = 2R/c,   μ = B/Tc,   fb ≈ μτ = 2BR/(cTc).

Key idea: for a stationary target and an ideal linear up-chirp, dechirping converts a tiny propagation delay into a beat-frequency difference. Range changes the delay; chirp slope converts that delay into beat frequency.

Try this: increase Tc while holding B and R fixed. The beat frequency decreases because the chirp slope decreases, even though the physical range has not changed.
